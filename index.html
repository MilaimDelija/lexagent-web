How it works

Three lines of code.
Full legal coverage.

Wrap any agent action. LexAgent classifies the legal risk, anchors the audit trail to the blockchain, and blocks the action if it exceeds your threshold.

your_agent.py — before & after 
# Before LexAgent — no visibility, no compliance
def approve_loan(applicant_id, amount):
    return lending_service.approve(applicant_id, amount)

# After LexAgent — 3 lines, full EU AI Act + GDPR coverage
from lexagent import LexAgentSDK
lex = LexAgentSDK(api_key="lxa_...", frameworks=["EU_AI_ACT", "GDPR"])

def approve_loan(applicant_id, amount):
    lex.decision("Approve loan", {"id": applicant_id}, {"amount": amount})
    return lending_service.approve(applicant_id, amount)
